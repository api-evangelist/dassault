CAD to CAM digital continuity seamlessly flows design data from CAD into CAM without file exports, manual rework or lost engineering intent, enabling teams to work from the same product information across design and manufacturing. This eliminates workflow friction like missing updates and NC programming delays while delivering up to 80% faster programming, 90% reduction in design change impact, 30–40% better machining efficiency and 30% higher part quality for a more scalable manufacturing operation.
