While heavy customization in manufacturing software creates rigid systems that delay updates and adaptations, configurable MES and MOM platforms allow companies to rapidly adjust workflows and scale across operations without lengthy IT development cycles. By prioritizing configuration over custom code, manufacturers protect their upgrade paths while maintaining the operational agility required to adapt quickly.
