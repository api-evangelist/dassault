High-tech manufacturing is facing an aging workforce and rising ergonomic risks, making physical stress on workers a major vulnerability. To solve this, companies are moving ergonomics from a reactive safety check to an upfront digital design discipline, using virtual simulation and immersive training to prevent injuries before the factory floor is even built.
